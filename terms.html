English

When you write to Sherkhan Tattoo on Instagram, some short, factual answers and appointment reminders come from the studio's assistant, using texts the artist wrote and approved. Prices, health questions, the address and anything unusual are answered by the artist personally.

  • An answer from the assistant is information, not a booking. A session is booked only when the artist confirms it.

  • You can ask to talk to the artist directly at any time, and ask us to delete your data.
  • The assistant is built and operated for the studio by Codru Atelier (codruatelier@gmail.com).
